About Fishy Studios

Fishy Studios is a studio made by Pumkinfish (Kind of one of the only developers, also the owner). It makes games on Unity, and Roblox. They range from 2d games to 3d games.

Most of the games are built around the fictional "Pumkinfish Species"

Current engines/platforms:

  • Unity
  • Roblox Studio
